About Sphita

Our Story

Sphita was founded with a singular focus: to solve the data bottleneck in modern AI development. We recognized that while model architectures are widely shared, high-quality, curated datasets remain heavily gated. We are here to change that.

We are a community-driven organization consisting of researchers, developers, and AI enthusiasts. We believe that true progress in artificial intelligence comes from radical collaboration and shared resources.

Our Open-First Philosophy

We are committed to open-sourcing approximately 90% of our codebases, datasets, and models. For extremely large or complex datasets, we implement a staged release strategy (e.g., releasing an initial 60% partition) to ensure rigorous quality control and safety checks before the remaining data is published.
